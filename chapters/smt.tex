\mkchapter{Formalization of SMT-LIB 2.7}
\label{ch:smt}

This chapter formalizes a targeted fragment of SMT-LIB 2.7~\cite{smtlib2.7} sufficient
to serve as the target language of BEer.
Its structure deliberately mirrors that of \Cref{ch:b}: each section has a direct
counterpart on the source side, and the differences are precisely those imposed by
the gap between the set-theoretic B language and the many-sorted first-order logic
of SMT-LIB.
\Cref{sec:smt-syntax} introduces the syntactic categories: SMT types, a concrete
first-order representation of terms that closely follows the surface syntax of
SMT-LIB~2.7, and the PHOAS representation used for semantic reasoning.
\Cref{sec:smt-typing} develops the type system for both representations and
establishes the structural properties used in the correctness arguments.
\Cref{sec:smt-semantics} gives the denotational semantics in the shared ZFC
universe of \Cref{ch:zflean}, and proves type adequacy and totality for
well-typed terms.
This chapter forms the target-language contract against which the
encoder of \Cref{ch:beer} is verified.
