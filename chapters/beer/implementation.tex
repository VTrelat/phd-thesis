\label{sec:beer-implementation}

The rules of \Cref{sec:beer-encoding-rules} deliberately hide the state threaded by the executable Lean encoder.
This is an expository simplification, not an omission from the formal proof: the state properties are part of the complete mechanized invariant, and their preservation is proved for every encoding operation.

\subsection{From declarative rules to a pure encoder}
\label{sec:lean-implementation-architecture}

After decoding an Atelier~B proof obligation file, \beer{} runs a pure state-and-error computation ({\small\leancodeptr{Encoder}{Encoder/Basic.html\#Encoder}}) that produces the SMT-LIB script.
Its state contains the running SMT typing context~$\Gamma$, a monotone supply of fresh names, and the declarations and assertions emitted so far.
The proof of \Cref{sec:beer-soundness} exposes only the semantic projection of the invariant; the Lean proof also preserves the clauses relating these state components.
We elide the corresponding weakest-precondition calculations and retain only one representative example of the scoping discipline they establish.

\subsection{Binders, names, and contexts}
\label{sec:binders-names-contexts}

The fresh-name supply and the typing context have deliberately different lifetimes: the supply remains monotone throughout the run, whereas~$\Gamma$ is extended on entering a binder and restored on leaving it.

The function case of {\small\leancodeptr{defaultSpecM}{Encoder/Loosening/Rules.html\#defaultSpecM}} illustrates the discipline:
\begin{leanlisting}
| .fun α β, t => do
    let x    ← freshVar α
    let body ← defaultSpecM β (.app t (.var x))
    eraseFromContext x
    pure (.forall [x] [α] body)
\end{leanlisting}
Here $x$ is needed in~$\Gamma$ while the body is constructed, but it is bound in the returned formula and must not remain visible as a free constant afterwards.
The cast machinery follows the same pattern for its local binders.
In the universal case, helpers generated beneath a source binder are likewise moved from the global declaration stream into the target quantifier, as prescribed by the \encruleref{rule:beer-bounded-universal} rule.
The preserved invariant therefore ensures both that bound names do not escape and that a name already used by an emitted declaration is never issued again; the lower-level proof details play no further role in the mathematical development.
