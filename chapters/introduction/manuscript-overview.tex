\section{Manuscript overview}
\label{sec:manuscript-overview}

\inlremark{This section is still WIP.}

This manuscript deliberately adopts a mathematical register: it presents the
encoding and its metatheory through definitions, typing judgments, and
denotations rather than through the Lean code that realizes them. The artifact
behind the thesis is nonetheless an executable, formally verified program, and
some implementation concerns are inseparable from its correctness argument.
Chief among these is the \emph{monadic} structure of the encoder: it threads a
typing context, allocates fresh names, and accumulates the emitted SMT script.
We make this structure explicit where it bears on a design decision or a proof,
chiefly in
\Cref{sec:lean-implementation-architecture,sec:binders-names-contexts}, and keep
the state it manipulates implicit elsewhere.


\paragraph{Notation.}
We fix two notational conventions used throughout the thesis.
We write \(\regnotation{\coloneq}\) for \emph{definitional
assignment}---binding a name to a term or giving the body of a definition---and
distinguish it from propositional equality \(=\) and from the metatheoretic
symbol \(\regnotation{\triangleq}\), which is read ``is defined as'' and used in
displayed mathematics.

When a proof component is unused or irrelevant in a given context, we write
``\(\regnotation{\irrelprf}\),'' following Lean notation.
