\mkchapter{Formal Foundations of the B Mathematical Language}
\label{ch:b}

This chapter develops a formal account of a curated fragment of the B mathematical
language, covering the types, expressions, and predicates that appear in B proof
obligations.
The presentation proceeds in the following sections.
\Cref{sec:b-syntax} introduces two complementary syntactic representations: a concrete,
first-order syntax faithful to the raw proof-obligation format, and an abstract
syntax suited to semantic reasoning.
\Cref{sec:b-typing} defines the typing relation over both representations, annotating
terms with their B types and preparing the ground for the semantic layer.
Finally, \Cref{sec:b-semantics} gives a denotational semantics for the abstract syntax,
built over the ZFLean set-theoretic framework of \Cref{ch:zflean}; it also introduces the
well-definedness predicate (\Cref{sec:b-wd}), which captures the semantic side conditions
imposed by partial B operations and delimits where the denotation is total. This semantic layer
is the foundation on which the certified encoding of \Cref{ch:beer} rests.
